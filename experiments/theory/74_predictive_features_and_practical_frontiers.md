# Predictive features, conditioning and a practical frontier

The current evidence does not support a generic failure of differentiation.
Frozen reference/fast forward and every-parameter gradient contracts pass,
including the native gesture adapter. The earlier count-language credit64
comparison failed its quality gate; late value-map differentiation was repaired
but did not improve the strongest original addressed result. These outcomes
remain evidence about their protocols and information paths, not proof that
all deeper features are unlearnable.

## An information contract before a gradient contract

Let X be the observed prefix, Y the supervised future label, and S=f_theta(X)
the state exposed to the prediction head. For a sufficiently flexible head
trained to population optimum, minimum cross entropy is H(Y|S), and the gap
to a prefix-optimal predictor is

    H(Y|S) - H(Y|X) = I(Y;X|S) >= 0.

This is a definition of a predictive information bottleneck, not a claim that
our finite heads attain either optimum. Correct gradients cannot recover
evidence absent from S; a failed finite readout fit also cannot prove the
evidence absent. Separate retention, decoder expressiveness, credit reach,
finite-data estimation and optimizer conditioning when diagnosing a failure.

For features Z1,Z2, I(Y;Z1)=I(Y;Z2)=0 can coexist with I(Y;Z1,Z2)>0.
The balanced relation task realizes this interaction. The terminal uniform
address proof in note70 further exhibits an uninformative read law and a
zero-gradient symmetry at a scoped fixed-context zero-residual initialization.
This is an attainable stationary point, not a global architecture no-go.
The native context can itself break the premise: three of four frozen
seed7/8 interventions retained high accuracy without delivered values.

## Correct credit can still be statistically misplaced

For a mixture p_y=(1-e)c_y+e q_y with an unchanged expert c, the learned
component's log-probability derivative is multiplied by

    r_y = e q_y / p_y.

When counts already explain the target, r_y is small. Increasing graph reach
or width then need not improve quality. Adam can cancel a constant gradient
scale approximately, but cannot turn example-dependent r_y into equal useful
credit, restore an absent information path, or resolve interaction symmetry.
No conclusion follows from gradient magnitude alone. The completed language
stratification and matched minimal controls must accompany this derivation.

## What the new real region tests

The calibrated time-aware DVS count model reaches58.85% accuracy, whereas
nonlinear controls reach73.44% at the selected NLL and74.48% at maximum grid
accuracy on the same984/192 causal first-second gestures. Thus predictive
relations beyond that particular class-count model are practically useful;
these numbers neither isolate temporal order from spatial nonlinearities nor
show that the native model learns them. No official test has been opened.

The unchanged native p16/depth2/H2/pool2 candidate now fits eight fixed passes.
It must earn quality against the strongest preserved controls. Its persistent
state has720 tensor bytes and15,523 parameters at this shape, but these
figures omit process, event graph, scoring, traffic and optimizer overhead.
They establish available structure only. Actual training/inference work and
wall/RSS must be included before a practical resource claim.

A development quality gain at higher work adds a measured candidate frontier
point; it does not establish total superiority. Lower isolated inference
arithmetic at comparable development quality remains a projection until
matched causal sequential latency/storage and independent confirmation pass.
One can be optimistic about trainability without predicting any of those
results. The next decision comes from completed fits, not an assumed ceiling
or a promised win.
