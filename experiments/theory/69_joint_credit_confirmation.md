## Interpretation and limits

The restricted query suffix/count representation is identical within every
four-example group, including symbol totals, so its conditional target entropy
is one bit. The full prefix contains two ordered bits. Protected latest-outcome
addresses retain both without encoding targets. Learned races and bilinear
message interaction can decode their relation. Neither individual bit predicts
the balanced target: E[Y S1]=E[Y S2]=0 while E[Y S1 S2] is nonzero in sign coding.
An individual-route value linearization therefore need not give the same
direction as differentiating the loss over a pair. Note68 gives the exact
conditional categorical-risk derivative. This experiment tests that risk and
credit intervention; it does not isolate every estimator detail or prove an
universal superiority of joint learning.

Explicit protected evidence and conditional nonlinear losses fix a concrete
information/credit path on this task. They do not prove a generic autograd
regression, deep abstraction in the native core, useful historical producer
credit, or a mathematical ceiling for architecture adaptation. Other count
addresses can retain these bits too; the bound concerns query-count inputs.
Inference must score all occupied keys. Joint fitting pays C squared losses
and cannot be described as free learning behind two sparse deliveries.
