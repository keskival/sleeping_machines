# Learning routes, contents and useful capacity: decision synthesis, 2 October

The objective is a trainable sparse temporal substrate whose available memory
and processing capacity can exceed its active work. Quality, learning cost and
execution cost must improve together. Counts are appropriate controls in their
region; improving a dense diagnostic alone does not demonstrate this thesis.

## Three bottlenecks, three different repairs

1. Predictive evidence must reach the readout. If S=f(X), the population
   flexible-readout gap is H(Y|S)-H(Y|X)=I(Y;X|S). A finite probe cannot certify
   this optimum. Retention interventions, initial-reservoir controls and proper
   readout fits can separate access from a merely weak decoder (note74).
2. Credit must teach realizable choices AND the functions producing their
   values/state writes/times. Wrong-sign local teachers are established at
   specific sites. Correcting one categorical component leaves other teachers,
   future hard timing jumps and finite training exposure unresolved.
3. More state must have learnable processing. Private parameter maps receive
   fewer selected examples when the pool grows. Under the explicitly assumed
   parametric c*d/n rate, untied maps cost c*sum d_u/N, whereas shared maps cost
   c*d/N. This is a conditional exposure model, not a universal neural sample
   complexity theorem. Private state and shared processing should be tested
   separately from routing/key identity and useful timescales.

## Conditional branch derivative: exactness versus exposure

At a fixed entering prefix, first time T and future draw, define legal
actual-write/full-suffix losses F_i(theta,T), probabilities pi=softmax(s),
and R=sum_i pi_i F_i. Away from changes in later hard topology,

    dR = sum_i pi_i dF_i + sum_i pi_i*(F_i-R) ds_i.

This decomposes branch/content credit from choice credit; shared parameter
Jacobians still add both paths. Smooth clock and future-support boundary terms
are separate. No claim of exact full-model temporal credit follows.

The factual estimator dF_W with W~pi is unbiased for sum_i pi_i dF_i under
these assumptions. No-grad alternate forwards therefore do NOT by themselves
prove a missing expected content derivative. They fail to directly teach the
loser's content on that realization; a rare branch with probability pi_i sees
approximately N*pi_i selections, and estimator variance/exposure may be poor.
The actual native suffix still contains local routing surrogates, so the
smooth fixed-topology derivative and full expected derivative must be audited
separately. Do not treat observed nonzero gradients as useful credit evidence.

For any factual W and a full-support alternative I~q(.|W), one candidate
branch-credit estimator is

    h_hat = pi_W*dF_W + [pi_I/q(I|W)]*dF_I.

Its conditional mean over I equals sum_i pi_i dF_i for EVERY W. The weights
are detached when taking the branch derivatives; categorical credit is added
separately. With two candidates this enumerates the branch derivative exactly.
With the note86 proposal, alternative weights are bounded by1/(1-epsilon),
but derivatives themselves need not be bounded. Adding an unweighted factual
gradient to this estimator would double-count factual content. A second
backpropagation is paid; this is a proposed comparison, not an implemented fit.

A forced alternative delivers/writes I while retaining the ACTUAL minimum
time of factual W. A differentiable replay must direct payload/write gradients
to I and the current minimum-time derivative to W. Reusing a surrogate which
assigns both to the forced winner corrupts this experiment. Establish separate
winner identities and every-parameter VJP contracts before implementing it.

## Next decision sequence, with finite work

First perform a fitting-prefix-only frozen audit at prespecified early/late
sites and both layers, using saved weights and a small fixed alternative pool.
Separate categorical fidelity, smooth branch VJPs and clock derivative from
future topology jumps. Measure mean/variance and parameter-group alignment
over independent draws; audit global-clipping effects without selecting on
development labels. Label finite-draw estimates and conditional references.
Compare predicted directional effects with finite perturbations only where
the fixed-topology margin permits them. Do not call forced routing a realizable
emitter parameter update.

If content exposure/fidelity is the remaining bottleneck, test the branch
estimator above at the SAME selected nodes as choice-only credit, retain native
timing, and first establish legal writes, factual identity, separate current
time ownership, independent VJP and optimizer/recovery/work contracts. Then one
24/8/two-pass smoke. If evidence instead identifies retention or clock noise,
repair that path rather than spending fits on this conjecture.

Only after the measured smoke cost, preregister a small matched quality screen
with initialization/draw/data/update controls and a separate equal-work
comparison. Reuse the two saved choice-only seeds where compatible. A new
backward-work budget must be declared before its pilot; the existing1.50 gate
cannot be relaxed retrospectively. Require independent-seed confirmation before
full984-fit comparisons; report all attempts and whole-fit/per-target costs.
No automatic third seed, epoch extension or pool/depth growth after failure.

Integrate tied-map capacity only after its independently owned comparison
completes. Larger paired pools have bounded replay count, not free discovery,
keys, losing proposals, optimizer or low variance. Smooth receive windows and
silence-burst primitives stay deferred until a causal irregular-time adapter
and integrated information/credit test justify them. Fixed50ms packets do not
establish natural-silence learning. Grow depth from a verified nested parent
only if the depth audit supports that independently owned protocol.

After a confirmed full-data quality gain, measure the practical frontier:
strong controls at matched causal data/quality, storage, one-thread sequential
latency, fitting work, discovery and traffic. Energy remains unmeasured until
instrumented. Official test and AWS large dense comparisons keep their existing
protocol. Stop a line whose repair fails its declared gate; retain evidence.

This prioritizes information about the bottleneck per unit of compute. It
supports cautious optimism about tractable mechanisms, not a probability of
supremacy or a promised benchmark gain.
