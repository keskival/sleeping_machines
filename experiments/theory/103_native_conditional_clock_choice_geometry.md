## Local pullback, not a full expected-risk gradient

For each selected two-candidate site define

    u = grad_theta(s0-s1), v = grad_theta(logsumexp(s)).
    F_choice = pi0*pi1 * u*u^T; F_clock = v*v^T.
    F_joint = F_choice + F_clock.

These are conditional entering-score sensitivities under the actual earlier
winners, receiver writes and physical times. Persistent contents still depend
on parameters through their genuine earlier writes/readout/mixing paths. The
two nonzero eigenvalues of this local metric are those of the2x2 Gram matrix
of sqrt(pi0*pi1)*u and v; no dense15523x15523 matrix is needed. Shared parameter
Euclidean cosine, normalized rank and information conditioning are separate:
sharp pi can reduce the small eigenvalue even if u and v are independent.

Retain blocks: receiver clock biases; keys/key-read/query maps; message/state
and transport maps; classifier. At an unclamped native site, the two local
clock-bias coordinates give u=(1,-1) and v=(pi0,pi1). Thus single-site rank
can be two already. Those same biases are tied across many events/inputs;
single-site controllability is not simultaneous independent control of every
history, and not proof contents can be changed without routing effects.

Negative cosine is not harmful credit by itself (note91). This study has no
target loss and no counterfactual return, so it cannot identify a bad update,
measure utility-weighted noise, or nominate a Fisher optimizer. Earlier routes
are conditioned rather than averaged; future unobserved histories are absent.
Actual native time paths must be restored in any subsequent learning proposal.
