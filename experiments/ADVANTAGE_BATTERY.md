# Battery for demonstrated quality/resource advantage

Start with the most plausible mechanism-specific wins, then recognized tasks.
The objective is better prediction per complete resource budget, including
learning. Pilot success, a benchmark Pareto advantage, frontier best accuracy
and measured physical energy are different claims. Keep every completed parent,
negative result and protocol revision. Do not promise that scaling wins.

## Promotion and measurement rules

1. Screen at one seed; promote at most two constructions per task, selected
   by dev quality/work Pareto evidence and a specific mechanism effect. Do not
   run every width/depth/seed combination. Preserve negative points.
2. Compare the selected construction with its exact parent at seeds6/7/8,
   identical fitting data/passes, and at least256 dev queries; repeat the
   original stronger512-query/eight-pass event protocol. Freeze choices before
   independent confirmation seed3201 and at least1,024 queries. Measure long
   gaps, jitter, missing inputs and source counts separately.
3. Initial engineering target: at least2x less COMPLETE work or traffic at
   predeclared noninferiority, or a clear accuracy/loss gain at equal work.
   This is a goal, not a predicted result. For final event classification use
   at most1 percentage-point accuracy loss and1% relative NLL loss as practical
   noninferiority margins, with paired uncertainty. For language use at most
   .02bpc loss, explicitly labelled near-quality rather than identical quality.
4. Whole-fit quality/work is primary; include hyperparameter selection budgets
   for each family, inference, memory, tail latency and optimizer exposure.
   Seed variation and whole-population/trial uncertainty are distinct. A
   one-population interval cannot support generalization.
5. Benchmark promotion requires timestamp-aware strong controls under the same
   data/quality boundaries, independent test evaluation and reproducible raw
   results. New Transformer/LSTM training remains AWS work. Logical FLOPs do
   not stand in for physical energy; tree FLOPs remain uncounted until audited.

## Analytical battery: pursue advantages before brute-force capacity

| Question | Derivation / falsifiable measurement | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Retain useful information during silence | Protected identity subspace, temporal semigroup and observation Gramian (§§354/366); long-gap labels invariant | Local preservation proved; whole-model gap invariance unproved |
| Shared statistical exposure with private state | N_private=q+S*p+S*D, N_shared=q+p+D (§355); measured optimizer/work and cross-source leakage | Explicit source addressing; does not make discovery free |
| Time communicates more than winner identity | Race arrival carries total-rate information; Fisher(log total rate)=1/race (§361) | Clock setting/noise/readout costs remain; not exact deterministic softmax aggregation |
| Pool conditionally sufficient messages | Precision/evidence, moment and identified-channel witnesses (§359), hierarchical posterior moments (§367) | Exact under stated assumptions; new learned-message variants conditional |
| Learnable freedoms match explanatory factors | Task-weighted Jacobian/latent-tangent alignment, reachable rank and conditioning (§358), cross-generator/receiver KL (§363) | Calibration probes and causal teacher adapters required; no universal cheap receptor |
| Hierarchical factors rather than layer count | Conditional-expectation multiresolution (§367); teacher factor-depth probes; generator-family cross-validation | Network layers are not automatically independent factor scales |
| Attention arithmetic savings versus compression | With equal width/projections, dense attention≈8D²+4KD+softmax; hard race≈8D²+2KD+aux. Score pairs remain. Without extra mechanisms the leading arithmetic ratio is bounded near2, not an asymptotic win | Attention sublayer only; training loser credit/optimizer charged separately. Bigger gains require smaller models, sparse capacity, traffic or hardware evidence |

After the immediate screen, admit one message addition at a time: calibrated
precision/count, role-aware moments, conditional low-rank interactions, then
multiple arrivals/window integration. Require non-redundant information,
observable gradients and held-out benefit per complete cost before combining.
Cross generators and receivers (native, attention, recurrent) to separate a
useful bias from a favorable synthetic teacher. These future cells are not
fabricated runnable queues. Chronological and hardware stages test the wider
architectural case; language accuracy alone is not its only potential value.
