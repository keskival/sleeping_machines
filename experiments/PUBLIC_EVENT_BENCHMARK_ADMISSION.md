## What counts as beating a benchmark

**Accuracy record:** full official population, exact dataset/preprocessing and
selection disclosure, current strongest verified comparable references and at
least three independent training seeds. A single best epoch on test, a private
split or a numerically better score against an uninspected abstract is not a
record. Report seed spread and paired per-example uncertainty with independent
speaker/session clusters for speech/gesture. Few clusters limit significance.

**Efficiency win:** predeclare noninferiority and a resource boundary, then
reproduce a competitive control with equal raw observations and tuning access.
The initial goal is >=2x lower *complete* work with <=0.02BPC worse text8 loss
or <=0.5percentage-point worse speech/gesture accuracy. These are campaign
goals, not selected benchmark standards or guarantees. Use one-sided paired
confidence bounds; at least three seeds. Record stronger and cheaper reference
points, not only the one we beat. A modest quality loss outside the margin is
a tradeoff, even if the work ratio is enormous.

Report training FLOPs (and convention), total fitted presentations, unique
examples, passes, optimizer work and total tuning cost. For inference report
per-example/per-event work together, key scores, value proposals/deliveries,
state/weights/working memory, setup, p50/p95 latency and same-hardware wall.
Charge decoding, packet assembly, selection, caches and readout. Actual energy
requires the same declared system boundary including idle and sensor/host costs
where relevant. A new efficiency curve on a public dataset is a **new measured
comparison**, not automatically a win on an existing energy leaderboard.

Early-prefix prediction, label-preserving silence sweeps and online adaptation
are *supplementary protocols*. Keep full official accuracy primary. Prefixes
must use only events available by the same fixed observation deadline for every
model; any learned stopping policy is fitted on TRAIN/DEV and includes deadline
misses. Silence/order manipulations are not the official dataset leaderboard.
