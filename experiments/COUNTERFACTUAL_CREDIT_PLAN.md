## Conditional alternatives

If usable histories are missing after credit improves, compare a larger
eligible associative pool with fixed inference winner count and matched state
capacity/data. Log coverage, discoveries, scored keys, trained values, replay
horizon and joint choice order separately. An early broad shadow schedule may
shrink on measured variance/coverage; preserve a full-support exploration floor
and an unchanged sparse inference protocol. No free all-pool scoring claim.

If useful multi-arrival interactions are absent, integrate the tested smooth
window or silence-burst primitive with native races/state and explicit optional
event credit. First establish a causal irregular real adapter, scheduler,
deadline/latency and derivative contracts. Fixed50ms DVS packet schedules do
not test meaningful natural-silence learning. Popcorn hard merge/split/horizon
credit requires actual alternatives or an explicit stochastic boundary model.
