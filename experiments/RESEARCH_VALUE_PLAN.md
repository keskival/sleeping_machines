# Experiments that most reduce uncertainty about Sleeping Machines

## Decision and priority — 1 October 2026

The user has redirected the main effort toward native temporal/sparse strengths,
rather than requiring the construction to resemble Transformer attention.
Preserve the completed KV comparisons, useful temporal-attention proofs and
previous structured-task wins. Their value and limitations remain visible.
No universal superiority is assumed, and an inefficient restricted variant
is not treated as a refutation of the full substrate.

The most valuable evidence closes a specific doubt with a reproducible quality
and resource comparison. Small tuning improvements alone do not do that.

| Priority | Doubt to resolve | Experiment and decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Can the integrated native construction learn useful order and elapsed-time representations? | Eight event blocks, two independent receiver heads, addressed persistent state and counterfactual learning; interleaved order and time-dependent signed-event tasks. Refit pathwise-credit and missing-time controls. Replicate promising results with three seeds. |
| 2 | Does the native construction generalize on language without importing an attention scaffold? | The same native event core, token content adapter, no per-position KV bank; disjoint text8 development, fixed passes, small-to-larger data ladder, train/dev gap and full fitting/inference work. | 3 | Is dormant state useful at bounded active work? | Occupy 4, 16 and 64 independent stream populations, not padded unused modules. Hold query/event training budgets, active depth/heads and candidate pool fixed. Score every stream, count all key scores, losing proposals and optimizer work, and measure memory. |
| 4 | Does the advantage survive real irregular data and online change? | Adapt the validated native core to the existing chronological market event protocol first, including marked waiting-time/survival likelihood and predict-before-update; then speaker-disjoint SHD. Compare tuned timestamp-aware controls with equal input information on AWS. |
| 5 | Is there a repeatable quality/resource Pareto advantage? | Three-seed quality-versus-whole-fit-FLOPs and inference-work curves on selected real tasks; separate matched-quality and iso-budget comparisons, with confidence intervals and tuning budgets. Larger language runs become valuable once small integrated fits establish a useful path. |
| 6 | Does the physical substrate save energy? | Export a validated event workload for FPGA/ASIC work. Measure whole-system energy, memory traffic, physical clocks, routing overhead, latency and native learning updates. A CPU arithmetic projection cannot close this doubt. |

Priorities 1–3 overlap: the first native core and task adapter support all three.
New Transformer/LSTM training remains reserved for AWS. This local CPU host
runs one trainer at a time, under unique one-job queues and the RSS watchdog.
No remote AWS connection, hardware board or energy measurement is assumed.

## First executable native-event campaign

`AddressedEventHeads` retains incoming content, learned computational delays,
independent receiver heads, analytic rotating/decaying persistent state,
learned channel mixing and losing-route counterfactual credit. It omits
per-position KV attention. Each observed stream address selects its own pools
and persistent deep context. An address is input information; labels never
select a module. A source waits only for its own preceding output when needed;
other sources have independent progress. There is no periodic hidden-state scan.
CPU loops still simulate the event schedule and numerical clocks.

Each independent population supplies three signed content events per source,
followed by one explicit causal query per source. All source states are occupied.
Physical timestamps are irregular. The order label is the ordered pair of the
last two signs (four classes). The timing label is the sign of a known two-mode
signed temporal trace (two classes); it genuinely depends on elapsed time.
These are synthetic capability tests, not real-world benchmark supremacy.

Use payload 16/head, depth 8, two heads, two candidates per observed source;
512 query targets / 2,048 observed events per fitting pass; eight fixed passes;
256 development queries. Adam uses 64 queries per update at lr .003. All source
populations complete before an update, with their graphs retained to supervision;
no delayed query is silently denied its producer credit. Fixed data seeds 1201
and 2201 permit paired refitted controls. Development chooses minimum NLL;
seed 6 pilots do not read the independent confirmation seed 3201.

Order: full contracts and small accounting fits for both tasks; order pilot;
native language 2K pilot; timing pilot; refitted no-counterfactual order and rank-time timing controls;
conditional native language data scaling, occupied-capacity 4→16→64 and seed 7/8 replications. Promoted seed
runs additionally score 1,024 independently generated confirmation queries once
under the fixed protocol. Query outputs precede loss/updates. Confirmed scores
never choose a model or learning rate. Bootstrap by independent populations;
report seed dispersion separately. Sparse per-source recurrent controls are
required alongside Transformers later, since addressing itself is not unique.

Pilot gates are decision rules, not statistical supremacy tests:

- Order native development accuracy ≥80% and clearing addressed history loses
  at least 20 percentage points; otherwise diagnose content/credit before capacity.
- Timing native development accuracy ≥75%; erased timestamps and the refitted
  rank-time control identify whether elapsed time contributes. A three-point
  gain over the refit is the initial replication threshold.
- Capacity 16/64 needs the order gate, numerical/memory smoke at its actual
  capacity, and no more than five points of degradation at each stage. Report
  per-event and per-query work, available/occupied state and parameter exposure.
  Training visits per address change with population at fixed total budget;
  include this limitation rather than inferring a quality gain from capacity.
- Replicate an eligible mechanism with seeds 7/8 at identical settings. Preserve
  failure/variance and all controls. A promoted claim needs confirmation
  evidence, not only a selected development score.

Refitted controls run even when a full pilot misses its capability gate, so
weak performance still yields diagnostic evidence. The executable campaign
defers any potentially day-long 131K language extension until after capacity
and independent event replications.

The native language branch is a separate test with a token content projection
into one stream's addressed core. Its context is persistent state, not a claim
of storing or reproducing a full KV cache. It must retain parallel content
channels, actual depth and counterfactual learning. Width/depth/data changes
need measured work and generalization gaps. Do not delete the old stronger
receiver/structured evidence while the new branch is pending.

## Current evidence and what it does not settle

The older H2 text8 variant reaches 3.733 development bpc after 2K fitting
characters and 3.490 after 8K; its final online training loss is 2.691 while final
frozen development is 3.510. It learns and benefits from data, with substantial
small-data overfitting. Its 3.82M parameters see little data. These points do
not establish a trajectory to dense-control or frontier supremacy.

Historical write credit improves the 2K score to 3.724/full strength or
3.722/quarter strength from 3.733, while fitting work rises from 22.753 to
23.464/23.469 GFLOPs and K/V float storage rises 50%. Both miss the predeclared
.02 bpc promotion gate; their larger definitions remain unrun. Keep this small
positive effect and its costs visible. More attention/credit tuning is not the
first uncertainty-reducing experiment under the new research direction.

## Reporting and systematic operation

Commit plan, models, protocols and uniquely named queues on main before long
fits. Freeze source fingerprints, keep tmux command/logs, and validate completed
files before report publication. Write numerical errors and gate failures beside
the original results. Update the appendix with completed quality, training data,
whole/per-target fitting work, inference/event and inference/query work,
capacity/activity and persistent bytes. Never fill pending cells with estimates
of achieved quality. Resource projections and measured physical energy are
separate ledgers. Report real-data and hardware stages as prepared/awaiting a
validated adapter or provisioned host until they actually execute.
